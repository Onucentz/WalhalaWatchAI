Built on real national data, not vibes

Every severity score and dashboard claim can be benchmarked against Nigeria's own official statistics — so judges and investors see verifiable ground truth behind the AI, not a black box.

National Bureau of Statistics

Nigeria's authoritative source for GDP, CPI/inflation, and sector-level economic data, published by the federal statistics agency.

nigerianstat.gov.ng ↗
  • Weight severity scoring against sector GDP contribution (e.g. power, transport)
  • Track inflation/CPI alongside cost-of-repair complaints

Nigeria Data Portal

State-by-state statistical indicators — population, water access, transport — aggregated for cross-checking citizen reports.

nigeria.opendataforafrica.org ↗
  • Compare reported water outages against official water-access rates by state
  • Localise multilingual defaults to each state's language distribution

World Bank Open Data

Internationally comparable development indicators — useful for the investor-facing view of impact and scale.

data.worldbank.org/country/nigeria ↗
  • Frame WahalaWatch's reach against national infrastructure benchmarks
  • Support pan-African expansion pitch with comparable country data
